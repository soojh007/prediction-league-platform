# Managed Prediction League Hosting

For office pools, fan clubs, schools, sports bars, and community groups who want to run a private football prediction league without managing servers, code, or third-party sports data subscriptions.

---

## 🚀 What We Provide

1. **Fully Hosted Platform**:
   - Hosted on [predictionleague.site](https://predictionleague.site) with high availability and SSL security.
   - Zero DevOps or technical setup required from you.

2. **Automated Live Sports Feeds**:
   - Pre-configured live fixture schedules and automatic match-day score syncing for competitions like the Premier League, UEFA Champions League, and World Cup.

3. **Dedicated Organiser Admin Account**:
   - Access to the Organiser Portal at `/organiser/login/`.
   - Select which match fixtures to include in your competition.
   - Customize scoring rules (exact scores, correct outcome, goal difference).
   - Generate private invite links and codes for your members.
   - Post announcements and track participant predictions.

4. **Player Experience**:
   - Dedicated private leaderboard for your group.
   - Mobile-friendly interface for players to submit predictions and check live rankings.

---

## 📩 How to Request a Managed League

To set up a private league on our hosted platform:

1. Send an email to [`hello@predictionleague.site`](mailto:hello@predictionleague.site) with:
   - **Tournament / Competition** (e.g. Premier League 2026/27, UEFA Champions League)
   - **League Name** (e.g. "Acme Corp Fantasy Pool", "Red Devils SG Fan Club")
   - **Estimated Number of Players**
2. We will provision your private league and email you your **Organiser Admin Account** credentials within 24 hours.
